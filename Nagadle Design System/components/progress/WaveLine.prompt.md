The squiggly progress connector — sits between `StepDots`, or alone under a heading as a playful rule.

```jsx
<WaveLine tone="done" width={120} />
<WaveLine width={120} />
```

Notes
- Keep `wavelength` and `amplitude` identical everywhere on a screen; the waves must read as one continuous line.
- Tan = ahead, yellow = reached. Never lime or red.
