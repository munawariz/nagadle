Navigation link, always underlined and always arrowed — use instead of a bare `<a>` for back/next movement between screens.

```jsx
<ArrowLink href="#home">Back</ArrowLink>
<ArrowLink variant="chip" direction="forward" href="#archive">Archive</ArrowLink>
```

Notes
- `plain` on cream surfaces; `chip` when the link sits over the wave pattern or a moss card.
- The underline is part of the brand — do not remove it.
